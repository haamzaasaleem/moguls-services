How we handle consumables

Amazon and Walmart both reject food that arrives too close to its date. These are the checks that keep yours moving.

  • Expiry date check at receivingDates are read and recorded on every carton the day it arrives.
  • Lot trackingEach lot number is logged, so a recall or a buyer question can be traced to the exact units.
  • Oldest stock ships firstPicks always pull the earliest date on hand.
  • Variety pack buildingSnack boxes, sampler packs and multipacks built from your single items. See bundle pricing
  • Expiry alerts by messageWe message you at 90, 60 and 30 days before stock expires.
  • Photos of received cartonsYou see what arrived, and what condition it arrived in, before anything ships.
